 How does Impossible Cloud compare to other providers?
 Unlike many global providers, Impossible Cloud offers true European digital sovereignty with data protection under a single legal framework. Our proprietary, vertically integrated technology stack ensures enterprise-grade performance and efficiency.
At the same time, our transparent pricing model has no hidden fees, making costs predictable and easy to control.
 Where is Impossible Cloud headquartered?
 Our headquarters are in Hamburg, Germany, underscoring our commitment to operating under strict European data protection laws.
 Who owns Impossible Cloud?
 Impossible Cloud is privately owned by its co-founders, Dr. Kai Wawrzinek and Dr. Christian Kaul, together with leading European venture capital firms and investors.
 When was Impossible Cloud founded?
 Impossible Cloud was founded in 2021. Since then, we have grown rapidly, serving several thousand customers and building a strong partner network across Europe.
 Why might I not have heard of Impossible Cloud before?
 Impossible Cloud was founded in 2021 and is still a young company in the cloud infrastructure market. Since then, we have established ourselves with several thousand customers and a strong partner network. Our initial focus was on the DACH region (Germany, Austria, and Switzerland), but we now operate across major markets and continue to expand.Many renowned media outlets have already reported on our growth – you can find a selection in our Media Room 
 Why the name Impossible Cloud?
 We chose the name because our platform solves challenges that traditional providers left unresolved. What once seemed impossible – full control over your data at a fair price with high performance – is now possible with Impossible Cloud.
 How does the pricing for Impossible Cloud work?
 Our pricing is transparent and predictable, with no hidden fees. You only pay per terabyte of storage used. Customers can choose between a flexible pay-as-you-go plan or reserved capacity plans with long-term discounts
‍
Bare metal services are billed based on resource usage. Pricing is calculated per GPU card-hour. All applicable pricing is shown before deployment.
 How do you provide high-performance storage at such an affordable price?
 We combine innovative architecture with deep expertise. Our use of erasure coding is more efficient than traditional replication, delivering durability with less overhead. We pass this savings onto you directly.
In addition, our proprietary metadata system ensures low latency and instant data access, even at scale.
 What is your policy on data retention and deletion?
 You have full control over your data. There is no minimum retention period, so you never pay for deleted data and remain fully independent. Data is automatically removed from our systems once your trial ends or you terminate the service.
 Does Impossible Cloud charge for egress?
 No. We do not charge for egress, meaning the transfer of your data out of our object storage. This allows you to access your stored data without additional costs. As a general rule, monthly egress should not exceed the volume of actively stored data.
 Does Impossible Cloud charge for API calls?
 No. Unlike many other providers, we do not charge for API calls or operational requests. Our pricing is simple and predictable — you only pay for the storage you use.
 Is there a minimum monthly cost or commitment?
 No. Impossible Cloud has neither a minimum storage duration nor a monthly commitment. You only pay for the storage you actually use, making it a flexible option even for unpredictable workloads.
 How can I monitor my usage and costs?
 You can monitor your Object Storage usage and costs in real time via the Impossible Cloud Console. It provides clear insights into stored data and expenses.Information about Bare Metal GPU usage and billing is available within the respective documentation.
 Can I create geo redundant backups?
 Yes. Impossible Cloud uses erasure coding to distribute data across servers, so failures do not result in data loss-
Additional protection methods, such as backups, further enhance data security.
 What kind of performance can I expect from the service?
 Impossible Cloud provides an always-hot storage model with consistently low latency and high throughput. Internal benchmarks show that our solution can be significantly faster than comparable services in the market. Performance may vary by workload, and you can explore this directly by starting a free trial 
 How durable is Impossible Cloud Storage?
 Impossible Cloud Storage is designed for 99.999999999% durability (known as “11 nines”) over a year, meaning objects are highly unlikely to be lost.
We still recommend following best practices such as using versioning, access controls, and regular backups to protect against accidental or malicious deletion. Bucket-level compliance features can further prevent unwanted data removal.
 Is Impossible Cloud fully S3 compatible?
 Yes. Our storage is fully compatible with the AWS S3 API, making it easy to integrate existing tools and workflows. This ensures a seamless switch from other providers without the need to adapt applications or processes.
 Do you offer Object Lock (WORM) functionality for immutable storage?
 Yes. Our object Lock provides WORM (Write Once, Read Many) functionality, ensuring that data cannot be changed or deleted for a defined retention period. This feature is crucial for compliance requirements and effective ransomware protection.
 What integrations and backup solutions do you support?
 Most Impossible Cloud customers integrate our storage in conjunction with solutions such as Veeam, Acronis, MSP360, Nakivo, and Hornetsecurity, with whom we offer deep or native integrations.
Our ecosystem continues to grow with additional technology partners. See our partner section for more details.
 Can I migrate my data from another provider to Impossible Cloud
 Yes. Migration is seamless thanks to full S3 compatibility. For reserved capacity plans we also include a migration offer with dedicated support at no extra cost.
 Are GPU resources dedicated per customer or shared?
 Impossible Cloud bare metal GPU servers provide full access to dedicated NVIDIA DGX systems. GPU resources are not shared with other customers, and no virtualization layer is used.
 How is bare metal GPU usage billed?
 Bare metal GPU services are billed based on usage and the pricing is calculated per GPU card-hour. All applicable pricing is shown before deployment.
 In which regions are bare metal GPU servers available?
 Bare metal GPU servers are available in the same supported regions as Impossible Cloud Storage. Available deployment locations are shown during configuration.
 Who is the contractual partner and how is support handled?
 Impossible Cloud is the contractual partner and support is provided through our service framework. Applicable service terms and SLA details are defined in the relevant documentation.
 How do you guarantee data sovereignty?
 Impossible Cloud is a German company operating fully under European law. We run our own technology stack and do not rely on external data sub-processors, ensuring your data is protected from foreign jurisdictions such as the US CLOUD Act.
 Where is my data stored?
 Your data is stored in ISO-certified data centers located in Germany and other European regions.
All locations meet strict EU data protection requirements and are designed for maximum security and compliance.
 How is my data protected at rest and in transit?
 Your data is encrypted during transfer and while stored in our data centers. We apply multi-layer encryption using industry-standard algorithms to ensure confidentiality and integrity at every stage.
 What security certifications do you have?
 Impossible Cloud is ISO/IEC 27001 certified as a company and our information security management system (ISMS) is audited according to this standard.
Our services are delivered through data centers that are certified to ISO 27001, SOC 2, and PCI DSS standards.
 How can I manage access to my data?
 Impossible Cloud includes identity and access management (IAM) features such as role-based access control (RBAC) and multi-factor authentication (MFA). These tools let you define detailed permissions and ensure that only authorised users can access your accounts and data.
 Who has access to my data?
 Only you have access to your data. Our security model is designed so that even Impossible Cloud employees cannot access your content.
Access is fully controlled by you through secure authentication and permission management.
 Can you assist with data migration and project-based support?
 Yes. Impossible Cloud provides migration assistance and project support for Object Storage transitions to our platform. Our team of experts offers guidance to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
 What support channels are available?
 You can reach our support team via the request form on our website or by email at help@impossiblecloud.com from your business domain.For general inquiries, you can also use the contact form .
 Is there technical support available for partners and resellers?
 Yes. As a channel-first company, Impossible Cloud prioritizes collaboration with partners and resellers. We provide dedicated resources and responsive technical assistance to help you serve your customers and grow your business.
 What are the support hours and response times?
 Our support system is available 24/7/365 with automated escalation for system issues. Tickets submitted by email are usually answered by the next business day. Response times depend on the severity of the request.
 Do you offer Service Level Agreements (SLAs)?
 Impossible Cloud Object Storage is designed for very high object durability. The applicable SLA terms are defined in our Terms and Conditions.Bare metal GPU services are covered by the applicable service terms and SLA provisions as defined in the relevant documentation.
 How can I provide feedback or request a new feature?
 We welcome your feedback. You can submit a support request with the severity set to "Low" to share suggestions or request new features. Your input helps Impossible Cloud continuously improve its services.
